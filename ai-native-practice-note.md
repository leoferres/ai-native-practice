This work is an AI-native research program. AI may participate at every stage of the work, including the formation of questions, analysis, criticism, programming, and writing. I do not treat AI involvement as a defect requiring apology. I do, however, accept responsibility for everything I publish. I will disclose uses that affect the evidential or methodological status of a result, document important failures, correct errors publicly, and avoid manufacturing output merely because output has become cheap. The experiment is not whether AI can produce more text. It is whether a person working closely with AI can develop forms of inquiry that neither could produce alone. For a more detailed version of this note, see [AI Native Practice](https://github.com/leoferres/ai-native-practice/blob/main/ai-native-practice.md).
